## External destinations

Ask Morrow may route visitors to public links already present on the website (for example LinkedIn, Spotify, YouTube or Amazon), but v1 does **not** crawl or ingest those external profiles.

## Routing rule

When a question is better answered by the site than by a generated explanation, prefer:

**short orientation → relevant route → optional explanation**

rather than reproducing large amounts of source text.
