Engine requirements and regression cases: typed events and effects with controller, zone, object types/subtypes, token status, face/door, timing, costs, resolution counts, once-per-turn gates, replacement effects and conditional creation. Enchantment entry vs full Room unlock are distinct events; one door unlock is not necessarily full unlock; first/second/third resolved ability per turn produce different effects, fourth has no effect. Multiple listeners to one event are parallel, not causal links. Replaying a permanent can generate a new entry event; life loss is not lifegain or damage. Tribal token copy retains abilities while modified characteristics apply; nontoken-only listeners must reject token entries. Amass creates a token only if no Army exists; otherwise counters/type change, not a new body; upkeep effects need ordering and actual board state. Graveyard exile can both enable copies and reduce resources for other recursion; replacement exile prevents death triggers. Card types, creature vs spell, targeted vs untargeted, mana and timing restrictions matter. Use independent positive/negative fixtures and preserve unknown/unsupported rather than infer absent capability. Official reference: https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/duskmourn-house-of-horror-release-notes and https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/the-lord-of-the-rings-tales-of-middle-earth-release-notes.
