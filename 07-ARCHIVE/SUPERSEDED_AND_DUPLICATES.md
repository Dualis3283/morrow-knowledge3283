## Housekeeping policy

A record should move here when:
- a newer verified state supersedes its “current” claim;
- it duplicates a closure already represented by a canonical record;
- its next action was completed elsewhere;
- it is a recovery snapshot rather than an active project;
- retaining it helps explain history but using it for current execution would cause drift.

Do not delete failure evidence simply to make the system look cleaner.
