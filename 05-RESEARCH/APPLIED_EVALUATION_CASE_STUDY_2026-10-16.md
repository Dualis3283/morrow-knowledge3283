## Findings
1. A well-structured answer is not proof every methodology stage was executed.
2. Context must govern optimisation; faster or stronger is not necessarily better for the owner's goals.
3. Explicitly separate observed input, confirmed validation, hypothesis, opinion, and unknown.
4. Do not present qualitative numerical scores as quantified measurements.
5. If verification is deferred, label it **NOT OBSERVED** and describe how it can be tested.
6. Preserve low-friction conversation: no-change is a legitimate outcome.
