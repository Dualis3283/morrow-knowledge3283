      MORROW / DECK LAB

      A place to learn
      your deck’s rhythm.

      Built around your website’s black-and-gold visual language and Compleated Loyalty playmat artwork.

      Play manually, or run repeatable, scripted opening tests. The opponent ranks threats using public battlefield information: combat pressure, mana value, legendary status, and recognized engine or synergy text. It does not inspect your hand.

      First version boundaries

      Automated tests cover a limited set of costs, effects and combat rules. Review card coverage before trusting a result. Unsupported spells remain manual. Commander tax, replacement effects, complex triggers and complete priority windows are not a complete rules implementation.

      Decks and table state are saved on your device. Importing a card sends its name to Scryfall; simulation runs locally without an AI model.

      The sample decks are small demonstration fixtures, not reconstructed versions of your personal Commander lists.
