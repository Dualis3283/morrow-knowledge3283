# Morrow Strategic Direction — 14 October 2026

**Status:** Owner-approved strategic decision. **Priority:** P0, overriding objective.

## Decision
Morrow's primary goal is to develop an **open, model-independent, interoperable and independently verifiable methodology suitable for worldwide adoption** by AI assistants, agents, developers and organisations. Existing Morrow projects and workstreams remain valid but are secondary and should be evaluated for their contribution to this mission. The new priority supersedes old strategic ordering, not validated history or safety gates.

**Universal compatibility** for a clearly defined class of AI systems is a testable engineering goal. **Universal adoption** is a voluntary long-term aspiration, neither achieved nor guaranteed.

## Guiding method and safeguards
Context → Evidence → Pattern → Interpretation → Action → Verification → Lesson.

“Morrow helps understanding compound without allowing history to become a cage.”

Historical context is evidence, not destiny. Preserve human agency, the right to challenge and revise, consent-based data exchange, privacy and deletion controls, explicit uncertainty, provenance, independent verification, and safety/permission boundaries.

## Proposed architectural layers — not yet validated
1. **Morrow Core:** versioned open methodology, scope, non-goals and conformance criteria.
2. **Morrow Bridge:** portable evidence/context/decision/lesson schema with provenance and permissions.
3. **Morrow Runtime:** open reference implementations and model/platform adapters.
4. **Morrow Verify:** reproducible independent tests of continuity, recurring failures, evidence separation, verification, agency and efficiency.

Potential interoperability with MCP, AGENTS.md and A2A is to be investigated, not assumed.

## Evidence and unknowns
**Confirmed:** The project owner approved the new strategic hierarchy on 14 October 2026. Earlier project workflows provide potential case-study material.
**Unknown:** cross-model portability, measurable impact, implementation cost, generality, independent adoption, governance and conformance.
**Existing boundary:** Phase 6 19/20 workflow baseline remains incomplete/unfrozen. No unsupported improvement or global-adoption claims.

## Next sequence
1. Draft Morrow Core v1 scope, normative rules and failure modes.
2. Specify a portable, auditable and privacy-conscious Context Bridge data format.
3. Demonstrate transfer across at least three AI implementations including a local model.
4. Compare matched tasks with and without Morrow; publish positive, negative and inconclusive results.
5. Release developer guidance and reference tooling; seek independent review.
6. Approach standards communities after reliable benefit is established.

## Priority and continuity rules
Prior website, deck planner, app and creative work are supporting/secondary, not silently cancelled. Keep their statuses and existing QA, privacy, staging and production gates. Reconcile Next Action fields in Notion Working State and Decision Log according to Pipeline Reconciliation v1.

**Reversal criteria:** explicit owner change of direction or transparently reviewed compelling evidence; never infer irreversible commitment from past preferences.

**Handoff:** On resumption read this document, the current Notion Decision Log and Working State, and then relevant project-specific sources. First deliverable is Morrow Core v1 and a measurable conformance/evaluation plan. This checkpoint records intent, not successful implementation or adoption.
