# Morrow Visual System

## Cross-project direction

The current cross-project visual baseline is derived from the public website and Deck Evolution Planner work.

### Hierarchy

- **Serif display:** major titles, chapter moments, conceptual statements.
- **Inter / clean sans-serif:** body copy, interface text, controls, metadata.
- **Serif italic:** transitions, reflective lines, connective statements.
- **Warm restrained gold:** accent, hierarchy, emphasis.
- **Dark editorial base:** high contrast without excessive framing.
- **Intentional whitespace:** rhythm and eye-navigation rather than filling every region.

## Design rules

- Prefer hierarchy over decoration.
- Prefer a few strong visual anchors over many boxes.
- Use recurring visual language across website, documents, planner assets, and project presentations where it improves continuity.
- Preserve legibility before atmosphere.
- Separate artwork from mechanically/factually authoritative text when accuracy matters.
- Do not let an aesthetic reference silently override project-specific usability.

## Compleated Loyalty influence

Compleated Loyalty contributes:
- biomechanical/porcelain architectural atmosphere;
- black-oil contrast;
- strong focal hierarchy;
- full-art presentation;
- controlled white text over art where readability is proven;
- symbolic differentiation between cards, tokens, and emblems.

These are references, not mandatory styling for every Morrow output.

## Owner-approved Morrow emblem — 2026-11-13

The canonical mark is the isolated golden Gemini-inspired arched M over a round Tiger's Eye cabochon. The owner selected this final treatment after removing the outer ring, cardinal coils, chain, background and additional words. No text is part of the emblem; Morrow remains the wordmark when a label is required. Use it as a shared brand asset, with the transparent background preserved and the aspect ratio unchanged. The pendant's Bones & Skin principles remain a design philosophy rather than mandatory logo iconography.
